PROJECT_04
Python GNN Computer Vision Deep Learning
Intelligent Traffic Congestion Prediction & Control using Vision and GNN

An AI-powered traffic congestion detection system that combines Computer Vision and Graph Neural Networks (GNN) to identify and analyze congestion on road networks in real time. The vision component processes traffic camera feeds to detect vehicle density, count, and movement — extracting meaningful visual features from live footage to determine congestion levels at each point.

Road intersections and segments are modeled as graph nodes, allowing GNN to learn spatial relationships between connected roads and propagate congestion signals across the network. This enables the system to accurately detect not just isolated bottlenecks but cascading congestion patterns across an entire road grid — demonstrating hands-on expertise in computer vision, deep learning, and graph-based AI applied to a real-world smart city problem.
